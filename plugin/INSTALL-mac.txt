HARMONIQUE MINI — install (macOS)

EASIEST: double-click "Harmonique Mini Installer.pkg".
  If macOS says it can't be opened: right-click (Control-click) the .pkg → Open → Open.
  It installs Harmonique Mini (VST3 + AU) and Harmonique Mini MIDI FX (AU, for Logic) into
  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins. It asks for your Mac password.

BY HAND (instead of the installer): copy the plugins, as they are (don't open them), to
     Harmonique Mini.vst3                 →  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
     Harmonique Mini.component            →  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
     Harmonique Mini MIDI FX.component    →  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
  then in Terminal, one line at a time:
     xattr -cr ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/"Harmonique Mini.vst3"
     xattr -cr ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/"Harmonique Mini.component"

Ableton Live: Settings → Plug-Ins → turn on "Use VST3 Plug-in System Folders" (and Audio Units),
  then hold Option and click Rescan. Harmonique Mini appears under Plug-Ins → VST3 → Oscillate Audioworks.
  Track 1: MIDI track with Harmonique Mini. Arm it.
  Track 2: your synth. MIDI From = 1-Harmonique Mini → Harmonique Mini (second menu), Monitor = In.
  Turn on Live's computer MIDI keyboard (M) and play A S D … — or use your controller.
